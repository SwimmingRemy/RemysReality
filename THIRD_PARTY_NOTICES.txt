Public-domain data - OurAirports, and the U.S. Census Bureau
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Three data files in the zip are built from public-domain sources:

  gameplay/srcl_earthcareer/airports.json
  gameplay/srcl_earthcareer/fuel_regions.json
  gameplay/srcl_earthcareer/house_prices.json

What is SwimmingRemy's in these files is the SELECTION, ARRANGEMENT, SCHEMA AND
ENCODING - the compilation - and that much is offered under the same terms as
the rest of the mod. THE UNDERLYING FACTS ARE PUBLIC DOMAIN and this
distribution asserts no right over them whatsoever: extract them, reuse them,
relicense your use of them as you wish. Each file names its own sources in its
"source" field, and those names must be kept with the data.

  airports.json      OurAirports (public domain), https://ourairports.com/data/
                     filtered to scheduled-service airports.

  fuel_regions.json  0.5-degree region cells derived from OurAirports
                     airports.csv (public domain). The US price levels are AAA
                     state averages (2026-11-06); the non-US levels are
                     approximate figures written by hand for a game, not a
                     dataset and not a live feed.

  house_prices.json  US values from the U.S. Census Bureau QuickFacts (American
                     Community Survey 5-year estimates), a U.S. government work
                     in the public domain. Non-US values are approximate figures
                     written by hand for a game.
                     NO ZILLOW, REDFIN OR MLS DATA IS USED OR INCLUDED.

  BeamNG.drive and everything that ships with it - the engine, the career

  RLS Career Overhaul - called at run time, separately licensed by its own

  BeamNG.Earth - the level this mod targets, by its own authors. Not bundled.
  Its place and building lookups draw on Overture Maps Foundation data, which
